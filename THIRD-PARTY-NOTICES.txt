services. NEXUS 1.3.0 does not use the Radio Browser directory or click API.
Official broadcaster pages: https://www.vojs.cn/
Official live configuration: https://lzlive.vojs.cn/info.json
Evidence and review scope: docs/RADIO_ALLOWLIST.md in the source repository,
or 电台白名单说明.md in the Windows distribution.
